A newspaper-style poster summing up the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026** in one image.

Lead story: a US jury convicts Huawei of trade-secret theft and bank fraud. Also covered: mines hitting four tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian ships, the Houthi advance on Aden and a drone strike on Riyadh's PIF tower, China's Shenzhou 24 launch carrying the first Pakistani astronaut to Tiangong, the ICJ's genocide ruling against Myanmar, 25 hostages held in Cartagena, and Senegal regaining the Africa Cup of Nations — plus an "Also today" strip of the day's other news.

`main.ts` copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The poster is drawn in `poster.html` with system fonts and flat CSS/SVG pictograms only — no photos, no external resources.
